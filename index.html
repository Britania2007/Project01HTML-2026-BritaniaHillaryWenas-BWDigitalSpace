About Me

Hi, I'm Britaniaa!

A fourth-semester Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Sam Ratulangi, from Manado, North Sulawesi, Indonesia.

I live between logic and emotion—where lines of code shape ideas, and music and movies give them meaning. As a technology student, I enjoy learning how things work, building simple projects, and exploring the digital world.

While coding is part of my journey, I'm more drawn to the creative and expressive side of technology—how digital platforms can tell stories, create experiences, and connect people in meaningful ways.

Beyond academics, I find comfort and inspiration in K-pop and cinematic stories that reflect real-life emotions.

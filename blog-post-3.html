Between Code and Calm: Stories I Live Beyond the Screen

ARTICLE 03 • BW DIGITAL SPACE

❮ BACK TO BLOG

Beyond the academic life filled with assignments, deadlines, and lines of code that don't always cooperate, I find my own way to stay grounded—through the simple things that bring me peace.

Music: My Daily Healing

One of them is music. For me, music is not just entertainment, but a form of healing from burnout. I can listen to music almost 24/7. In every lyric and melody, there is a sense of comfort—as if I'm never truly alone. Music helps calm my mind, reduce stress, and recharge my energy to keep going.

Movies: Tension & Warmth

Aside from music, I also enjoy watching movies, especially in the survival thriller genre. These kinds of films make me think deeper—how people face disasters, survive in difficult situations, and find solutions in uncertainty. They bring both tension and valuable lessons about critical thinking under pressure.

On the other hand, I also love animated movies like Cars and How to Train Your Dragon. These films offer a warm, simple, yet meaningful experience—reminding me that not everything has to be complicated to be meaningful.

K-pop: A World of Its Own

And of course, one of the biggest parts of my hobbies is K-pop. My journey started with EXO, the group that first introduced me to this world. My first bias was Park Chanyeol. From EXO, I learned about genuine friendship and trust.

As time passed, when EXO went on hiatus due to military service, I discovered NCT Dream. I instantly loved their music—it's mostly easy listening and carries uplifting messages for young people navigating life. My bias in NCT Dream is Lee Jeno.

Until now, both EXO and NCT remain part of my journey. In the middle of academic stress, they are always there—through melodies, lyrics, and music—becoming a constant companion during my most exhausting days.

“In the end, being a student is not only about studying and achieving grades, but also about finding the little things that keep us going. And for me, those things are music, movies, and the world of K-pop.”

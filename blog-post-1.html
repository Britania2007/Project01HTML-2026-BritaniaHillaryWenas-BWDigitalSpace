My Journey in Informatics Engineering

ARTICLE 01 • BW DIGITAL SPACE

❮ BACK TO BLOG

Entering the world of Informatics Engineering was not something I had planned for a long time. In fact, when I first started, I had no technical background at all—the only thing I knew was how to type in Microsoft Word. Surrounded by classmates who seemed far more advanced, I often felt left behind. There were moments of doubt where I questioned myself: “Do I really belong here?”

The First Year: Cultural Shock

The first year was quite challenging. I experienced a cultural shock when I was immediately introduced to subjects like mathematics, statistics, algorithm, and data. Everything felt overwhelming and fast-paced. It was stressful at times, but through that pressure, I learned how to persist. Step by step, I worked hard to catch up, and eventually, I managed to get through it with satisfying results.

The Second Year: Beyond Theory

In the second year, the challenges evolved. It was no longer just about understanding theories, but also about handling numerous projects—both individual and team-based. Through these experiences, I learned valuable lessons beyond technical skills: teamwork, time management, and how to deal with different personalities while working toward a common goal. I also began to realize that problem-solving is one of the most essential skills in the world of technology.

Now: Growing Even More

Now, in my fourth semester, I am in a phase where I want to grow even more. I understand that technology evolves rapidly, and if I stop learning, I will fall behind. That's why I am committed to studying harder, exploring new things, and continuously discovering my passion and core skills in informatics.

“I hope this journey will not only lead me to graduate on time but also inspire me to create new innovations and develop my creativity for the future.”
